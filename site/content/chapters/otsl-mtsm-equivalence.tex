%% title: Proving the equivalency of OTSL and a strict MTSM
%% category: theories

There are a few ways to prove they are equivalent, all kind of the same:

\pex \a \textbf{Mutual inclusion.} Prove that if a language is in OTSL, it is in MTSM, and vice versa.
    \a \textbf{Mapping?} Find a way to reliably get from an OTSL to an MTSM and vice versa.
    \a \textbf{Using the derivation.} Prove that MTSMs can only generate OTSL end states, and every OTSL end state can be generated by an MTSM.
\xe

Notes:

\pex \a One also needs to look at how multiple tiers can interact in both.
\xe
