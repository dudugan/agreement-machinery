%% title: Agreement as labelling
%% category: misc

I demonstrate that a variant of the TSL class known as ‘output-sensitive TSL’ (OTSL) can be made equivalent to a certain \textbf{labelling algorithm} for Merge, and that this labelling algorithm is the local instantiation of a certain formulation of Probe-Goal Agree. Building from an intuition present in \citet{graf2018}, I argue that this makes Agree essentially an epiphenomenon of Merge.

The mathematical properties of this labelling algorithm are crucial to what kinds of agreement patterns appear cross-linguistically. For example, a labelling algorithm which is associative predicts that the features of a non-terminal mother of [X[YZ]] always be the same as the features of a non-terminal mother of [[XY]Z], ruling out specific agreement patterns I term `rock paper scissors' patterns below.
