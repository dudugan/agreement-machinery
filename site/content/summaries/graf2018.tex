% Summary of graf2018: Why movement comes for free once you have adjunction

\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Motivation:} People think Merge is Strictly Local and Move is Tier-based Strictly Local because Move needs to ignore unbounded intermediate material.

\textbf{Contribution:} But once you add adjunction to the grammar, and give adjuncts category features that can themselves be adjoined to, then Merge becomes TSL, and requires basically the same machinery as Move. You're just matching an X+ feature and an X- feature, and collapsing the intermediate material between them.

\textbf{Impact:} This revitalizes Chomsky's intuition that Move and Merge are deeply similar.
\end{abstract}
