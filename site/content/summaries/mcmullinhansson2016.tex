% Summary of mcmullinhansson2016: Long-distance phonotactics as tier-based strictly 2-local languages

\begin{abstract}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Consonant harmony, disharmony, and blocking patterns are all TSL-2.
  \item This rules out pathological patterns predicted by the earlier Agreement by Correspondence framework:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Counting patterns
    \item Dissimilation which only happens non-locally
    \item Agreement by proxy (where two dissimilar segments may harmonize with each other if they are both sufficiently similar to a third element)
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}
